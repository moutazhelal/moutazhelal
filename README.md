### 🔬 About Me

I am a **PhD Candidate** at the **University of Würzburg**, bridging the gap between wet-lab biology and computational analysis. My research focuses on metastasis and therapy resistance in **breast cancer** and **multiple myeloma**.

I specialize in handling raw sequencing data and developing analysis pipelines for:
* 🧬 Single-cell RNA Sequencing (scRNA-seq)
* 🌌 Spatial Transcriptomics
* 🧪 Bulk RNA-seq & Metabolomics
